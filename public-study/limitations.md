# Limitations

This public study is intentionally incomplete.

## Exclusions

- Raw source documents and archive bundles are not included
- Private contact information is removed
- Direct source URLs and filenames are generalized or omitted
- Internal deployment details are not published

## Analytical limits

- Some findings are summarized at a higher level to preserve source privacy
- Exact counts may be rounded or grouped when needed
- Some region-specific detail is reduced to avoid source reconstruction

## Interpretation guidance

Readers should treat this repository as a public study and not as a full evidentiary archive. It is designed to explain the framework, not to reproduce every underlying record.
