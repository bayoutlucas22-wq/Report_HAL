# Overview

This study examines how a multi-region operational intelligence platform can be used to compare regulatory exposure, incident patterns, and contract footprint across several energy markets.

The public version is intentionally abstracted. It preserves the analytical structure, the high-level conclusions, and the presentation layer, while removing raw source bundles, private datasets, and direct source reconstruction material.

## Scope

- Regulatory and incident analysis across multiple regions
- Comparison of public disclosures and derived operational signals
- Sanitized reporting for study and presentation purposes

## Intended Use

- Academic or portfolio review
- Methodology illustration
- Public presentation of a complex research workflow
- Demonstration of dashboard and report-generation patterns

## What is omitted

- Raw CSV/JSON source corpora
- Source archives and embedded documents
- Credentials, tokens, and private deployment artifacts
- Direct identifiers that would allow source recovery
